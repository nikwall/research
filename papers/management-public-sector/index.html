Management and Performance in the Public Sector: Evidence from German Municipalities

Florian Englmaier, Gerd Muehlheusser, Andreas Roider and Niklas Wallmeier

IZA Discussion Paper 15676

Also CEPR DP 17670, CESifo WP 10060, TRR 190 DP 397

PDFIZACite

In short

A representative survey of German municipalities. Structured management varies widely, and municipalities that use more of it are more attractive to residents and firms.

What we find

  • Structured management practices vary widely across German municipalities.
  • The differences are not explained by state, type of region or population size.
  • Municipalities with more structured management score higher on how attractive they are to residents and firms.
