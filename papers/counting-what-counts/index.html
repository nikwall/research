Counting What Counts: Misaligned Performance Measures, State-Dependent Value, and the Prediction of Future Performance

Niklas Wallmeier

Ask for the draftCite

In short

Adding up output misleads when a unit of output is worth more in some states than in others. A framework for aligned performance measures, tested on football data; the first of three papers.

Results

  • Adding up output misleads when a unit of output is worth more in some states than in others.
  • In football, a goal at 0–0 turns a draw into a win and adds 2 points; at 0–1 it adds 1; at 3–0 it adds nothing.
  • An aligned measure values each goal by the points it adds to the result.
