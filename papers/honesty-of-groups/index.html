Working paper2024Honesty and cooperation in groups

Honesty of Groups: Effects of Size and Gender Composition

Gerd Muehlheusser, Timo Promann, Andreas Roider and Niklas Wallmeier

IZA Discussion Paper 16954

PDFIZACite

In short

An online experiment with 1,677 participants in 477 groups who talk by video chat. Larger groups lie more, all-male groups lie most, and a single woman already makes a group more honest.

What we find

  • Larger groups are more willing to lie.
  • All-male groups lie most.
  • A single woman in the group already makes it more honest.
  • How a group behaves cannot be fully predicted from its members' individual honesty.
