Article2024Organizations, management and work

Evidenz zur Verwendung moderner Managementmethoden in deutschen Gemeinden

Florian Englmaier, Gerd Muehlheusser, Andreas Roider and Niklas Wallmeier

ifo Schnelldienst 77(11), 34–39

ifo Schnelldienst 11/2024 (PDF)Cite

In short

In German, for policy-makers: the survey of German municipalities behind “Management and Performance in the Public Sector”, in the symposium “Kosten der Bürokratie – Reformen dringend geboten”.

What we find

  • How much structured management German municipalities use varies widely.
  • Most municipalities use relatively little of it.
  • Municipalities that use more of it score higher on how attractive they are to residents and firms.
