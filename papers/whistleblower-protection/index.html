Journal article2025Honesty and cooperation in groups

The Hidden Costs of Whistleblower Protection

Niklas Wallmeier and Timo Promann

Review of Law & Economics 21(2), 535–560

JournalCite

In short

A lab experiment on whether protecting whistleblowers, by making reports more likely, also reduces cooperation between managers and employees.

What we find

  • In a lab experiment, a manager and an employee can cooperate, and the employee can blow the whistle.
  • Protecting whistleblowers can deter wrongdoing.
  • When protection makes reporting more likely, it can also harm cooperation between managers and employees: a hidden cost of the policy.
