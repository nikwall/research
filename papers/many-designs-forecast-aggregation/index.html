A Many-Designs Study of 516 Algorithms Challenges the Predictability of Wisdom of the Crowd Forecast Aggregation

Christian König-Kersting, Yana Litovsky, Robert Böhm, Igor Grossmann, Jürgen Huber, Michael Kirchler and WoCCAP Consortium (One of the research teams in the WoCCAP Consortium; comments on and editing of the manuscript)

University of Innsbruck Working Paper 2026-05

PDFRePEcCite

In short

129 research teams submitted 516 algorithms to pool the same forecasts. Hardly any approach beat the others in a predictable way.

What we find

  • 129 teams independently built 516 aggregation algorithms, applied to forecasts by 1,182 people in economics, politics, climate and sports.
  • Most approaches did not predictably outperform the others.
  • The exception: algorithms that learned from past outcomes did better in politics and climate.
