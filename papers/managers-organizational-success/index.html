The Contribution of Managers to Organizational Success: Evidence from German Soccer

Gerd Muehlheusser, Sandra Schneemann, Dirk Sliwka and Niklas Wallmeier

Journal of Sports Economics 19(6), 786–819

JournalCite

In short

Bundesliga data, using the frequent moves of managers between teams: how much does the manager matter?

What we find

  • Teams with a manager from the top of the ability distribution win considerably more points than teams with one from the bottom.
  • A manager's estimated ability predicts the team's future performance.
  • Managers also shape how their team plays.
  • Teams whose manager used to be a professional player perform worse on average.
