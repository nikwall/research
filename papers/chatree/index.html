chaTree: An oTree Addon Allowing Face-to-Face Communication in Online Group Experiments

Timo Promann, Jan-Patrick Mayer, Gerd Muehlheusser, Andreas Roider, Eugen Tereschenko and Niklas Wallmeier

Economics Letters 252, 112349

JournalCite

In short

A free add-on that brings live video chat to oTree, so that participants in online group experiments can talk face to face.

What we find

  • Adds live video chat to oTree with little setup.
  • Records who talks when, so communication itself becomes data.
  • Free to use; the article is open access.
