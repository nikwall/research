Journal article2015Honesty and cooperation in groups

Gender Differences in Honesty: Groups versus Individuals

Gerd Muehlheusser, Andreas Roider and Niklas Wallmeier

Economics Letters 128, 25–29

JournalCite

In short

An experiment that compares the honesty of groups and individuals, and of men and women.

What we find

  • Participants report a die roll that only they have seen, and the report decides their pay; the design follows Fischbacher and Föllmi-Heusi (2013).
  • Deciding alone, men and women lie about equally.
  • Deciding in pairs, gender matters strongly: male and mixed pairs lie more than female pairs.
