Positions, Not Tasks: AI, Accountability and the Number of Jobs

Niklas Wallmeier

Ask for the draftCite

In short

A job is a position that carries accountability, not a bundle of tasks. A simple model predicts where AI cuts headcount and where it only changes how the work is done.

Results

  • A job is a position that carries accountability, not a bundle of tasks.
  • Where time limits the work, AI lets fewer people do it, and headcount falls.
  • Where someone has to answer for the result, the position stays; its holder delegates more of the tasks to AI.
