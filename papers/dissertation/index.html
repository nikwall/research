Essays in Organizational Economics

PDFCite

In short

PhD thesis, University of Hamburg; advisor: Gerd Muehlheusser. Four empirical essays in organizational economics, all since published: on honesty in groups, on managers, on performance feedback and on whistleblowing.

The four essays

  • Honesty: deciding alone, men and women lie about equally; deciding in pairs, male and mixed pairs lie more than female pairs (Economics Letters, 2015).
  • Managers: teams with a manager from the top of the ability distribution win considerably more points than teams with one from the bottom (Journal of Sports Economics, 2018).
  • Feedback: men perform better when compared with the best peer, women when compared with the average (Journal of Institutional and Theoretical Economics, 2021).
  • Whistleblowing: protection can deter wrongdoing, but when it makes reports more likely it can harm cooperation between managers and employees (Review of Law & Economics, 2025).
